Live Exports vs. Meat Imports — Austria

Austria's own live cattle exports next to its own beef & veal meat imports, __YEAR_RANGE__ — one side in animals, the other in tonnes, shown side by side rather than converted into one number. See Methodology and data for why these two figures can't be combined into a single comparable total.

Beef & veal meat imported

Headings 0201/0202, fresh/chilled and frozen, combined — no veal/beef split exists in trade data

Source: Eurostat Comext, dataset DS-045409. Live exports and meat imports are both Austria's own declarations, not independently verified against a partner country's records.

Why no combined total: converting live animals into an equivalent meat weight needs an assumed dressing percentage (carcass weight as a share of live weight), which varies by animal type and isn't one single verified figure — showing both numbers as reported avoids baking in that assumption.
